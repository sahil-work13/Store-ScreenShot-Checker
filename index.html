Store Screenshot Checker

Drop screenshots to check them

A ZIP, a folder or individual PNG and JPEG files. Everything is read in this browser tab and never uploaded.

Folder names like google-play-phone, ipad or feature pick the slot automatically.

What gets checked

This checks technical rules only: size, shape, format, transparency and count. Content is judged in human review. For example, iPad screenshots must show the app running on an iPad.

Files never leave your browser.
